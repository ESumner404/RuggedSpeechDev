## Beta App Description (up to 4000 characters)

Rugged Speech is a free communication app for children and young people whose speech is difficult, unreliable or
tiring: young children, autistic children, children with speech and language difficulties, and children who stammer.

Press big, colour-coded buttons to build a sentence and hear it spoken. It works with no internet and no account, and
nothing about the child ever leaves the device.

What is in it:
- Talk: a fixed board of core words and folders (Food, Feelings, Play, People, Places, Doing). Buttons never move.
- Keyboard with suggestions, saved phrases and a large "Show" screen.
- Feelings, Help and Calm, a traffic light that shows without words whether you want to be spoken to, and My body for
  pointing to where it hurts.
- My Day, First / Then, games, drawing and music from your own files.
- Your own photos and recorded voices.
- Ways of pressing: touch, switch scanning, hold-to-select, and it works with the iPad's own Eye Tracking, Switch
  Control, Voice Control and VoiceOver.
- Parent Mode behind a PIN for everything an adult changes, and School Mode for school staff.
- An Apple Watch app (a few things in one press) and, once linked in Parent Mode, Favourites and today's plan on the
  watch.

This is an early test build. The starter words have not yet been reviewed by a speech and language therapist, and the
app has not been tested with children. Please do not rely on it as someone's only way to communicate yet.

## What to Test (up to 4000 characters)

Thank you for testing Rugged Speech. This is the first build on a real iPad or iPhone, so the most useful thing you can
tell us is what does not work on a real device.

Please try, and tell us what happens:
1. First start: the short set-up, choosing a voice, and setting a Parent PIN (write the recovery code down).
2. Talk: press words to build a sentence, press Speak, and listen. Is the voice clear? Does it speak with the silent
   switch on?
3. Parent Mode (top right, then your PIN): add a photo for a button with the camera, and record a voice for a button.
4. Backup: Parent Mode, Backup, Save a backup, and restore it.
5. Print: Parent Mode, Print, and print a card.
6. Access: try your device's Eye Tracking, Switch Control or Voice Control with the app, and the larger text and
   Increase Contrast settings.
7. On an iPad, turn on Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility) and check the child cannot leave the app.
8. Apple Watch (optional): open the watch app, press Yes, and check it speaks.
9. Link (optional): Parent Mode, Access, Link with the Apple Watch and Apple TV.

Known gaps: the iPhone layout of Parent Mode is tight (use an iPad for set-up), a few screens still say "this device"
where they could say "this iPad", and the Apple TV app is not in this build.

Nothing is sent anywhere, so we only know what you tell us. Please email aac@communitystack.io, or use TestFlight's
"Send Beta Feedback" with a screenshot.
