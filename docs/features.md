# Features

Everything Rugged Speech Test does, in one list. For how to use each part, see [the whole guide](user-guide.md). For a quick start, see [parents and carers](getting-started-parents.md) or [teachers and staff](getting-started-staff.md).

Rugged Speech Test is a communication companion for children and young people whose speech is difficult, unreliable or tiring: young children, autistic children, children with speech and language difficulties, and children who stammer. It is a Windows desktop app that works with **no internet, ever**.

## Where it runs

- **Windows 10 and 11** (64-bit), as a per-user installer that needs no administrator password.
- **macOS**, as a disk image for Apple silicon and one for Intel Macs.
- The same app and the same features go out for both at every release.

## Principles that shape every feature

- **Works offline, always.** There is no network code. Every request outside the app is refused.
- **Nothing leaves the device.** No accounts, cloud, analytics, crash reports or update checks. Files leave only when an adult saves one.
- **Buttons never move.** Grids never reflow. Hiding or removing a word leaves an empty place.
- **The child's screen stays simple.** Everything configurable is behind a PIN.
- **Nothing is spoken unless someone presses something.** Prediction suggests; the person chooses.
- **No animation.** Movement is a sensory cost with no benefit here.

See [the principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) for the reasoning.

## Saying things

- **Talk board**: large colour-coded word buttons with folders (Food, Feelings, Play, People, Places, Doing and more), each holding nine things or fewer.
- **Sentence strip**: build a sentence, remove a word, clear all, press **Speak**.
- **Three ways a press can work**: say the word straight away, add it to the sentence, or both.
- **Reading a sentence out**: all together, with small gaps, or one word at a time with each word lit.
- **Keyboard**: large on-screen keyboard with suggestions that only ever suggest, and that learn the names of people and places.
- **Phrases**: Name, Address, Usual order, Register answer, and as many more as you need.
- **Starters**: ready-made phrases for the phone, a counter, school and meeting people.
- **Show**: the text in very large letters, silently, to hold up.
- **No-pressure mode**: nothing but the text box, the keyboard and Speak.
- **Favourites**: one-press buttons that speak straight away; added by holding a button.
- **Recent** (off by default): the last things said, with a time limit and a clear button.
- **Give me time**: one press says "I know what I want to say. Please give me a moment."
- **Quick Access bar**: six buttons that are always there, chosen by an adult, including speak-now buttons (Break, Question, Toilet, Finished, Say again). Help can never be removed.

## Words, pictures and voice

- **Drawn symbols**: bold, original pictures made for the app, or emoji instead. Picture and word, pictures only, or words only.
- **Your own photos**: for buttons, people and places, from the camera or a file.
- **Recorded voices**: a familiar voice says a button instead of the computer's.
- **Offline voices only**, with ready-made sounds (Usual, Gentle, Slow and clear, Bright, Young), and sliders for speed, pitch and volume.
- **Say it like this**: teach the voice how to say a name.
- **Colour has meaning**: button colour shows the kind of word, and an adult can change each (with a warning if two look alike).
- **Starter vocabulary** written for young children, with about thirty ready-made pages (snacks, playground, getting dressed, bedtime, doctor, maths, story time, and one for each season and celebration).
- **Boards editor**: add, hide, remove, rename, drag to swap, resize the grid (2 to 5), add folders, put a board back to the starter version.
- **My Pages**: pages built from scratch, shared as `.obf` files other communication software can read.
- **Profiles** (Home, School, Grandparents, Hospital): choose which page Talk opens to.

## My day

- **My Day**: today's activities in order with the current one marked, or a simple Now / Next / Later view.
- **Ask about an activity**: What's next? When? Where? Who with? Finished.
- **Change of plan**: announced once, with the old name crossed out beside the new.
- **Countdown warnings** (off by default).
- **First / Then**: two cards with a "first is finished" press.
- **Weekly routine**: a timetable entered once that fills in each week.
- **Visual schedules** printed as cards.

## Feelings, help and safety

- **Feelings** with how much (a little, medium, a lot), including what the body and senses need ("too loud", "need space").
- **Help** phrases, always one press away: "I'm lost", "I feel unsafe", "Someone hurt me" and more. They speak and stop; nothing is logged specially or reported.
- **Calm**: a breathing guide and a quiet-time timer.
- **My body**: point to where it hurts, say how it feels and how much. The figure can be a boy, a girl or non-binary, with skin, hair, a head covering (hijab, turban or kippah), clothes, a wheelchair, glasses and equipment such as hearing aids, a feeding tube or a pump. Always dressed; the private area is only ever "Under my pants".
- **Traffic light**: show, without words, whether you want to be spoken to (red, amber, green), with a marker on every screen and a full-screen "Show everyone".
- **Medical Info**: readable without a PIN, as text and as a QR code a phone camera can read, with no internet.
- **About me**: a one-page communication passport for new staff and relatives, printable.
- **Lost mode**: covers the screen with "This is a critical communication device. Please return it to …".

## Games and fun

- **Find the word**, **Snap**, **Rollercoaster** (build short sentences), **Draw**, **Jokes** (with your own), **Music** (songs from files you add, with playlists) and **Piano**.
- **Seasons**: words, Find the word and Snap for spring, summer, autumn, winter, Christmas, Easter, Halloween, Bonfire Night, Diwali, Eid and Hanukkah, with the one that suits today's date marked. An adult chooses which to show, changes the words, and adds others (Ramadan, Vaisakhi, Holi, Passover, Chinese New Year, Birthday, or their own).
- **Make a tree**: decorate a Christmas tree with baubles, lights, a star and presents; it is remembered.
- No scores against anyone, no clocks, nothing moves or flashes.

## Ways of pressing

- **Touch, mouse and physical keyboard** (arrow keys and Enter).
- **Switch scanning** with one or two switches, with an adjustable speed.
- **Hold-to-select** for eye gaze and head pointers.
- **Repeat-press suppression** for a double-firing finger or switch.
- **Text size** up to twice (boards scroll rather than hiding buttons), **high contrast** (light or dark), **low-arousal colours**, **reduce motion**.

## Looks

- **Favourite colours**: pink, orange, red, yellow, green, turquoise, blue or purple wash the whole app.
- **Other looks**: Standard, Calm, Sunny, Forest, Cream, Night, Yellow on black.
- **Make your own**: background, writing, outlines, bars and highlight, with a readability check.
- **Name the device** ("Lucy's device") so it is clear whose it is.

## Learning

- **Word stages**: bring words in gradually, with held-back words leaving an empty place.
- **Focus words**: a blue outline on the words being practised.
- **Clear the sentence after it is spoken**, if that suits the person.
- **Word counts** (off by default): how often each word is pressed, and which are never pressed.

## School Mode

A separate side for school staff, with its own button next to Parent Mode and its own **School PIN**, both switched on afterwards in Parent Mode.

- **Today**: the day at a glance, with a quick note.
- **Pupil and school**: school details, key adults, the speech and language therapist.
- **Safeguarding**: steps to follow and the designated safeguarding lead.
- **Timetable**, **Lesson pages**, **Vocabulary**.
- **Targets** with review dates, and **Notes** by kind (something noticed, went well, an idea, to follow up).
- **Activity** (off by default): counts by hour, day, week and month, saved as a spreadsheet only when you choose.
- **Reports**: a printable **handover sheet** and a **review report**.
- **Classroom set-up**: the school top bar, a typical school day, and an automatic close after a set time.
- **School guide** inside School Mode.

## Parent Mode

Behind a four-digit PIN with a recovery code, in a grouped menu with the whole guide built in. Sections: User guide, User, About me, Medical, My body, Boards, People, Places, My Pages, Quick Access, Profiles, My Day, Music, Jokes, Seasons, Access, Look, Learning, Targets, Notes, Activity, Reports, School, Lost mode, Backup, Print, General.

## Keeping it safe

- **PINs** stored as salted hashes; the keypad makes you wait after five wrong tries.
- **Backup** is one file you control, optionally protected with a passphrase (AES-GCM). Nothing is backed up automatically.
- **Print** cards, a sentence-strip template and a visual schedule for use without the computer.
- **Security hardening**: a strict content policy, every outside request refused, a sandboxed window with a narrow checked bridge, and disabled Electron features. See [the security notes](security.md).
- **No crash loss**: the sentence in progress and the page you were on are restored if the app closes.
- **Starts with Windows** (optional), keeps the screen awake while in use, single window, per-user install with no administrator password and a choice to keep or delete data on uninstall.

## What it does not do

- It does **not** lock the computer. A child can still switch to other programs.
- It does **not** stream music or connect to Spotify or Apple Music.
- It does **not** call, message or alert anyone, and it is **not** a safeguarding record.
- It does **not** find or lock a lost device, because it has no internet.
- It does **not** manage a class: one device, one child.
